# Research Work

Read project context, the latest journal, `.workflow/conventions.md`, and local
`workflows/research.md` if present. Establish the current objective and acceptance
criteria before expanding a calculation. Reuse the existing model and evidence.

Check prior work before making novelty claims. Record assumptions, units, bases,
observables, and competing explanations where they affect the result. Keep
exploration private; distinguish hypotheses, numerical evidence, and established
results. Validate with relevant limits, convergence checks, or independent
calculations rather than judging success from an attractive plot.

Leave reproducible commands and evidence beside experiments, summarize the current
conclusion and next action in project.md, and record decisions in the journal.
Read `.workflow/templates/guides/research.md` for coding, environments, and
bibliography defaults when relevant, and `.workflow/templates/guides/figures.md`
when making figures.
